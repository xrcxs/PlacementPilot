the filter problem

Most placement season resumes are killed by a script before a human ever opens them. ATS filters reject on formatting and missing keywords, not on whether you're actually a strong candidate. And even if you get through, most students walk into their first real interview having never done a proper mock one.

PlacementPilot fixes both failure points: an AI resume scanner that tells you exactly what an ATS bot sees, and an AI mock interview engine trained on real Indian campus interview patterns — SDE, Data, AI/ML, QA, and Support tracks — so your first tough question doesn't happen in the real room.
